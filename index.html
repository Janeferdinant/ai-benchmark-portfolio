Case 01 • Anomaly Resolution

Uncovering Simpson's Paradox in Multilingual Reasoning Benchmarks

Focus: Data Aggregation & Statistical Confounding

Problem: Model release reports indicated a 4.1% aggregate improvement across 14 languages. However, regional business stakeholders reported degradation in low-resource customer sentiment tasks.

Investigation: Disaggregated benchmark data by language tier. Discovered that a 20x higher sampling density in English (where the model improved slightly) concealed an average 8.4% drop across Indic and African languages.

Resolution: Implemented stratified population weighting and revised the release decision criteria, preventing a flawed production deployment.

Case 02 • Integrity Audit

Forensic Audit of Math Test-Set Leakage in Open-Source 70B Models

Focus: Contamination Detection & Validation

Problem: A new fine-tuned open-source model claimed higher GSM8K performance than frontier models 5x its size, raising suspicion of test-set data leakage.

Investigation: Executed an n-gram prefix memorization test and evaluated token loss across original questions versus numerically perturbed problem variants.

Resolution: Proved that model accuracy plummeted from 91.2% to 48.3% when arithmetic operands were perturbed, demonstrating rote memorization over reasoning. Produced a comprehensive forensic report for the safety board.

Case 03 • Methodology Refinement

Standardizing LLM-as-a-Judge to Eliminate Position & Verbosity Biases

Focus: Evaluator Calibration & Inter-Rater Reliability

Problem: Automated LLM judges displayed significant variance in win-rate evaluations, displaying a 64% bias toward the first-presented answer (order bias) and favoring longer answers regardless of correctness.

Investigation: Formulated a pairwise permutation protocol (swapping Order A/B) and trained an orthogonal ridge regression penalty to de-bias length.

Resolution: Increased agreement with senior human annotators from Cohen's $\kappa = 0.62$ to $\kappa = 0.89$, reducing evaluation pipeline costs by 70% while improving benchmark credibility.
