2. Benchmark Anomaly & Contamination Forensic Suite Data Integrity & Outlier Audit

Engineered an automated data integrity auditor detecting memorization vs. generalization through token perplexity shifts and multi-domain Simpson's Paradox audits.

  • Contamination Flagging ($Z > 2.5$): Measured $\Delta \text{PPL} = \text{PPL}_{\text{unseen}} - \text{PPL}_{\text{test}}$. Discovered that fine-tuned open models claiming >90% GSM8K collapsed to 48.6% when numerical operands were perturbed, demonstrating test-set memorization.
  • Simpson's Paradox Resolution: Disaggregated macro benchmark scores into stratified sub-domains (Law, Medicine, Math, CS). Showed that Model Beta led macro scores (+1.4%) solely through high-volume CS syntax while suffering severe regressions (-12.4%) in Legal reasoning.

Applied Industry Case Studies

Case 01: Uncovering Simpson's Paradox in Multilingual Reasoning Benchmarks

Problem: Candidate model claimed a +4.1% aggregate gain across 14 languages, but regional users suffered sentiment degradation.
Resolution: Discovered 20x higher English sampling masked an 8.4% drop in Indic/African languages. Enforced stratified population weighting.

Case 02: Forensic Audit of Math Test-Set Leakage in 70B Models

Problem: Open-weights 70B model claimed higher GSM8K performance than models 5x its size.
Resolution: Accuracy dropped from 91.2% to 48.3% on perturbed operands, confirming rote memorization. Delivered forensic report for leadership.

Case 03: Eliminating Position & Length Biases in Automated LLM Judges

Problem: LLM judges favored first-presented and excessively verbose answers.
Resolution: Formulated Order A/B swapping and an orthogonal length penalty, raising human agreement from $\kappa = 0.62$ to $\kappa = 0.89$.
