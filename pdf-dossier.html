Applied Industry Case Studies

Case 01: Uncovering Simpson's Paradox in Multilingual Reasoning Benchmarks

Problem: A model release candidate claimed a +4.1% aggregate gain across 14 languages, but regional teams reported degraded low-resource customer sentiment handling.
Methodology: Disaggregated performance by language cohort. Discovered a 20x higher sampling density in English masked an 8.4% drop in Indic and African languages.
Outcome: Implemented stratified invariant population weighting, halting a flawed release and establishing a new corporate multilingual benchmarking standard.

Case 02: Forensic Audit of Math Test-Set Leakage in 70B Parameter Models

Problem: An open-weights 70B model claimed higher GSM8K performance than proprietary models 5x its size.
Methodology: Executed an n-gram prefix memorization audit and compared token loss on original questions versus dynamically perturbed arithmetic variants.
Outcome: Proved performance collapsed from 91.2% to 48.3% on perturbed operands, confirming rote test-set contamination. Produced an executive forensic audit for safety leadership.

Case 03: Eliminating Position & Length Biases in Automated Evaluation

Problem: Automated LLM judges exhibited 64% first-token preference (order bias) and favored wordy responses.
Methodology: Formulated an Order A/B permutation swap protocol and fitted an orthogonal length de-biasing regression penalty.
Outcome: Elevated inter-annotator agreement from Cohen's $\kappa = 0.62$ to $\kappa = 0.89$, cutting human evaluation audit costs by 70%.
